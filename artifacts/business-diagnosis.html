Call cycle is each store’s scheduled CallCycle from Power BI (1 = weekly, 2 = every other week, …). Stores with no call in 120+ days are listed as “verify” and left out of past-due counts. Days past due = days since last call − cycle. Est. $ at risk = missed calls × the store’s average order per call. The downloadable workbook has the same data with one tab per Regional Manager.

Obsolete and invalid lines through the year

Obsolete lines are ordered but won’t ship. Rates bottomed in April–May and have roughly doubled since June, as discontinued items keep getting ordered without a replacement. The level is modest for Chain Service (about 4%) and material for Chain No Service (about 11%). October to date counts every October order line, including open orders not yet shipped; January–September counts shipped lines only.
